# Code of Conduct

ReconForge ERP is a professional open-source project for accounting, ERP, audit, and operations workflows. Contributors are expected to keep discussion respectful, factual, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different ERP, accounting, audit, and regional business practices.
- Discuss evidence, reproducible examples, and control objectives.
- Protect sensitive data and encourage anonymization before sharing examples.
- Welcome new contributors and explain domain assumptions clearly.
- Be honest about project maturity, limitations, and uncertainty.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Sharing confidential ERP exports, customer data, employee data, or financial records.
- Misrepresenting project adoption, audit conclusions, certification, or compliance status.
- Pressuring maintainers to accept unsafe changes.
- Publishing security details before responsible disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior puts contributors, users, or sensitive data at risk.
