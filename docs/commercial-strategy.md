# Commercial Strategy

## Market Problem

Companies with inventory, workshops, fleets, manufacturing, or service operations often struggle to prove that stock movements, GL entries, work orders, WIP, invoices, and purchase flows agree. Enterprise close platforms are powerful but may be expensive or broad. Manual Excel reconciliation remains common but is hard to govern.

## Target Customer Segments

- Mid-market companies using Odoo or SAP exports.
- Fleet maintenance companies.
- Dealership workshops and service centers.
- Manufacturing companies with WIP aging and material issue controls.
- ERP consultants and implementation partners.
- Internal audit teams.
- Accounting firms serving operational clients.

## Pain Points

- Month-end stock and GL differences.
- Manual matching of exported files.
- Weak evidence for auditors.
- Direct purchase-and-fit risk.
- Old-part returns not tracked.
- Work orders closed operationally but not financially.
- WIP remaining open indefinitely.

## Competitor Categories

- Enterprise financial close platforms.
- ERP-native reconciliation features.
- Data-quality platforms.
- GRC and audit evidence platforms.
- Spreadsheets and consultant-built macros.

## Differentiation

ReconForge ERP is narrow where it should be narrow: ERP export reconciliation, inventory-to-GL controls, WIP, work orders, spare parts, local processing, and audit evidence. It can complement larger systems instead of competing with every close-management feature.

## Open-Source Core

The core should remain fully useful: CLI, validation, reconciliation, rule packs, reports, evidence binder, anonymizer, synthetic generator, benchmark, Docker, and documentation.

## Paid Self-Hosted Version

A paid self-hosted edition could add:

- Reviewer assignments.
- Exception status workflow.
- User access control.
- Saved periods and recurrence tracking.
- Evidence attachments.
- Scheduled jobs.
- Advanced Studio UI.
- Enterprise support.

## Consulting and Implementation Services

Services can include ERP export mapping, Odoo/SAP implementation checks, close-process design, audit support, control-pack development, benchmark tuning, and staff training.

## Support Subscriptions

Support can cover installation, rule-pack updates, report customization, release guidance, and priority issue triage.

## Future SaaS

SaaS should be considered only for customers comfortable with hosted data or anonymized workflows. The SaaS value would be collaboration, scheduled review, managed rule packs, and team workflow.

## Pricing Hypotheses

- Community: free open-source.
- Support subscription: low annual fee for small teams, higher for enterprises.
- Implementation services: project-based.
- Self-hosted Pro: per company or per site.
- SaaS: per workspace or per reviewer when trust and feature maturity justify it.

These are hypotheses, not revenue claims.

Consultants can package mapping templates and control packs for industries they serve.

## Why Companies Would Pay Despite the Open-Source Core

Companies pay for time saved, implementation certainty, mapping expertise, reviewer workflow, support accountability, training, and audit-ready packaging. The open-source core builds trust; commercial services reduce operational risk and adoption friction.

## Positioning Statement

ReconForge ERP is the local-first audit intelligence layer for reconciling inventory operations with financial accounting.

## 90-Day Go-To-Market Plan

- Publish v0.2.0 with clear docs and sample outputs.
- Create short demo videos or screenshots from local data.
- Invite feedback from Odoo and accounting communities.
- Add two mapping-focused issues per target ERP.
- Publish a case-style walkthrough using synthetic data.

## 6-Month Growth Plan

- Build community-maintained mapping profiles.
- Add recurring-period comparison.
- Improve Studio review workflow.
- Package a self-hosted pilot for consulting customers.
- Create implementation templates and training material.

## Risks

- Becoming too broad and losing domain focus.
- Being perceived as another spreadsheet wrapper.
- Overpromising connectors before mappings are mature.
- Underinvesting in documentation for non-developers.
- Handling sensitive data without enough security guidance.

## Avoid Becoming Just Another Excel Tool

Keep the differentiators visible: deterministic matching, tests, rule packs, evidence binders, anonymization, benchmarks, local Studio, documentation, and open-source governance.

## Build Community Trust

Be honest that the project is early-stage. Publish working examples, accept domain feedback, keep sensitive data out of the repository, and avoid inflated adoption claims.
