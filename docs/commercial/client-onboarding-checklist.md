# Client Onboarding Checklist

## Pre-Pilot Questionnaire

- What ERP system and version are in scope?
- Which company/legal entity, warehouse/site, and period should be reviewed?
- Which reconciliation questions matter most?
- Who owns finance, audit, ERP, and operations sign-off?
- Are exports allowed to leave the client environment?

## ERP Export Checklist

- Stock/inventory movement export.
- General ledger/accounting line export.
- Product/item/material master export.
- Work orders or service orders if relevant.
- Purchase orders and invoices if relevant.
- Returns, customer, employee, equipment, or asset extracts if relevant.

## Data Privacy Confirmation

- Exports reviewed for sensitive data.
- Sharing authorization documented.
- Redaction or anonymization approach selected.
- Retention period agreed.
- External sharing limits understood.

## Mapping Checklist

- Canonical filenames agreed.
- Required columns mapped.
- Optional columns documented.
- Sign conventions reviewed.
- Period, company, currency, and warehouse/account scope confirmed.

## Review Meeting Agenda

- Pilot objective.
- Data scope and limitations.
- Mapping report.
- Exception summary.
- Evidence binder walkthrough.
- Review status decisions.
- Next actions and owners.

## Deliverables Checklist

- Management pack.
- Executive report.
- Dashboard.
- Evidence binder.
- Review register.
- Client handoff pack.
- Data privacy note.
- Manifest/checksum file if requested.

## Sign-Off Checklist

- Client validates export scope.
- Client reviews exceptions and assumptions.
- Client accepts limitations statement.
- Client confirms external sharing decisions.
- Follow-up owner and timeline assigned.

## Post-Pilot Next Steps

- Correct mappings or export layouts.
- Re-run for a second period.
- Compare recurring exceptions.
- Decide whether a monthly service or self-hosted support package is appropriate.
