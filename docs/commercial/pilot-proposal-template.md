# Pilot Proposal Template

## Objective

Run a local, export-based ReconForge ERP pilot to identify reconciliation exceptions, traceability gaps, and review workflow readiness for a defined ERP data scope.

## Scope

- ERP system: `[Odoo/SAP/ERPNext/Dynamics/NetSuite/Other]`
- Period: `[Month/Quarter]`
- Company/legal entity: `[Name]`
- Export files: `[List]`
- Control packs: `[List]`

## Required Exports

- Stock/inventory movement export.
- General ledger/accounting line export.
- Product/item/material master export.
- Optional work-order, purchase-order, invoice, return, customer, or equipment exports.

## Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data intake and privacy check | 1-2 days | Export checklist and scope confirmation. |
| Mapping and validation | 1-3 days | Mapping report and validation findings. |
| Reconciliation and evidence generation | 1 day | Management pack, executive report, evidence binder. |
| Review workshop | 1 day | Reviewed exception register and next actions. |
| Handoff | 1 day | Client pack and pilot summary. |

## Deliverables

- Mapping inspection report.
- Reconciliation outputs.
- Management pack.
- Executive report.
- Evidence binder.
- Review register.
- Client handoff pack.
- Pilot limitations summary.

## Assumptions

- Client provides local exports or sanitized sample exports.
- Exports are reviewed for privacy before sharing.
- ReconForge works from mapped exports, not direct ERP access.
- Client validates output interpretation.

## Limitations

- No audit opinion is provided unless separately delivered by a qualified auditor.
- No legal, tax, or regulatory advice is provided.
- No savings guarantee is made.
- No direct ERP connector is included.

## Data Privacy

All processing is local by default. The client is responsible for approving what files may be shared outside its environment.

Pricing hypothesis: `[Insert preliminary fixed-fee or time-and-materials estimate]`. This is not a public price commitment.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Sample exports validated or limitations documented.
- At least one reconciliation run completed.
- Outputs reviewed with client stakeholders.
- Exceptions classified or assigned for follow-up.
- Privacy note and handoff pack reviewed before sharing.
