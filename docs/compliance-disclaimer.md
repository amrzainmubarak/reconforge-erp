# Compliance Disclaimer

ReconForge ERP is a local-first reconciliation and control-review toolkit. It is not a substitute for professional judgment.

## Boundaries

- ReconForge does not provide legal, tax, accounting, audit, regulatory, or compliance advice.
- ReconForge does not certify financial statements.
- ReconForge does not issue an audit opinion.
- ReconForge is not a replacement for a licensed auditor, accountant, tax adviser, compliance officer, or legal adviser.
- ReconForge does not claim any compliance certification.
- ReconForge outputs must be reviewed by qualified personnel before management action, audit use, or external sharing.
- Local users and engagement owners are responsible for data privacy, data retention, access control, and sharing decisions.

## Appropriate Use

Use ReconForge to support:

- Local ERP export reconciliation.
- Exception triage.
- Evidence organization.
- Review status tracking.
- Consultant-led pilot discussions.

Do not use ReconForge outputs as the sole basis for financial reporting, regulatory filings, legal conclusions, tax positions, or audit sign-off.
