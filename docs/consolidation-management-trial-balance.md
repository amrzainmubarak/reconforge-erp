# Consolidation management trial balance v1

This artifact is a deterministic projection of a verified, balanced, non-posting consolidation worksheet. It is suitable for management review and evidence drill-down: every line retains its worksheet source references and the artifact carries a digest over canonical inputs.

It is not a statutory statement, legal-book posting, tax report, or independent assurance. It does not add acquisition accounting, goodwill, equity-method judgments, live rates, PostgreSQL persistence, or ERP/bank write-back.
