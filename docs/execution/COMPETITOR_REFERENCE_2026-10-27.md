# Competitor reference and evidence boundaries — 2026-10-27

ReconForge remains a local-first, open-source financial-integrity and reconciliation platform beside source systems. This comparison uses vendor documentation, not independent product testing, client outcomes, or a claim of overall superiority. Availability can depend on the cited product version/configuration. Source inspection and tests are evidence for a bounded implementation, not proof that an enterprise workflow is complete.

| Area | Official reference | ReconForge evidence at entry c085dbd1 | Open gap and acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role-based workspaces and discovery | [SAP Fiori spaces and pages](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_S4HANA_CLOUD/4fc8d03390c342da8a60f8ee387bca1a/f78a65b96121447e8b276c6dec94d637.html): spaces assigned by role, pages group apps, search exposes permitted destinations. | `apps/web/src/studio-routes.ts`, `apps/web/src/navigation-access.ts`, `docs/execution/STUDIO_NAVIGATION_2026-10-26.md`: 53 inventoried destinations in eight sections; permission-aware navigation, subject to server authorization. | Authentic company/workspace switching, live role-specific dashboards and full-product accessibility acceptance remain open. Validate authenticated journeys with real scopes; never derive access from hidden links alone. |
| Group matching and ambiguity | [Oracle match rules](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/account-reconcile-cloud/raarc/admin_trans_match_about_match_rules.html): one/one, one/many, many/one, many/many, tolerance and explicit ambiguity modes. | `reconforge/domain/grouped_matching.py`, `tests/test_grouped_matching_adversarial.py` implement bounded non-posting grouped decisions and ambiguity; `docs/adr/0381-dense-grouped-matching-adversarial-contracts.md` limits claims. | The existing v1 evaluator can produce a false match when Decimal context changes. A separate v2 experiment is under evaluation. Production adoption requires persisted version, replay, worker, UI and independent engine-parity proof; no existing v1 result is reinterpreted. |
| Bank reconciliation operations | [Microsoft Dynamics 365 matching rules](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/cash-bank-management/set-up-bank-reconciliation-matching-rules) describes ordered rules and an optional manual decision for ambiguous matches. [Worksheet](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/cash-bank-management/reconcile-bank-statements-advanced-bank-reconciliation) documents matched/unmatched review and journal workflows. | Existing matching and review surfaces are inventoried in `reconforge/reconciliation/`, `reconforge/workflow/`, and `apps/web/src/`; there is no complete equivalent bank-to-ledger production cycle established by current evidence. | Source-owned postings, reviewer actions, authorization and real PostgreSQL/Studio end-to-end proof remain required. Do not claim vendor parity from isolated engines. |
| Explainable unmatched transactions | [Oracle supported transactions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/account-reconcile-cloud/raarc/reconcile_trans_match_supported_transactions_about.html) retains supporting explanations through later matching and links them to period-end workflow. | `reconforge/workflow/`, `reconforge/evidence/` and bounded decision metadata exist. Current program's [cycle matrix](ENTERPRISE_CYCLES_2026-10-26.md) records disconnected source chains. | Prove a single authorized lineage from source through review, retained explanation, later match and period impact before claiming equivalent lifecycle coverage. |
| AI suggestions | [Oracle Transaction Matching Assistance](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/account-reconcile-cloud/raarc/reconcile_transaction_matching_assistance_about.html) describes prediction using manual history and published operational limits. [Microsoft account reconciliation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/general-ledger/account-reconciliation) documents optional suggested actions. | `reconforge/ai/` and governance-related API surfaces must be assessed independently; no equivalent model, training corpus, or measurable prediction quality is claimed here. | Evaluate grounded sources, authorization, injection resistance, model/version and human approval on synthetic or approved data. No autonomous financial posting or unapproved egress. |

Review date: 2026-10-27. This is a capability reference, not a certified benchmark, market ranking, independent product audit or proof that ReconForge outperforms a competitor. Controlled comparative trials would require equivalent scenarios, versions, datasets, environments, independently verified correctness, accessibility and measured time/memory/cost.
