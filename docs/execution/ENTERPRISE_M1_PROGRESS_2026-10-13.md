## Six domains and five guarantees

The six domain groups are manufacturing/supply, corporate finance,
commercial/CRM, people/HCM, projects/services/assets, and platform/UX/AI. The five
cross-domain guarantees are exact financial/operational integrity; identity,
privacy and isolation; atomicity/concurrency/replay; immutable evidence/audit;
and recovery/capacity/resource efficiency. Evidence is scoped to each actual
source and fixture. Wave5's earlier sales/procurement/returns/assets/FX evidence
is retained, and is not reclassified as new current-head acceptance.

CRM opportunity/service lifecycle, MPS/full MRP/scheduling, comprehensive HCM,
country payroll/tax, statutory financial reporting, controlled enterprise AI,
large mixed workloads, and independent multi-region HA/DR remain incomplete.
All countries remain the target. Proven activated statutory country packs are0;
generic tax/currency metadata is not a legal localization. No full ERP,
production readiness, legal compliance, competitor superiority,1M mixed
transactions or monetary cost per transaction is claimed.
