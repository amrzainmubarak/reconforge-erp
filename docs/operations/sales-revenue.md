# Governed service sales

The `/sales-revenue` Studio workflow uses a configured PostgreSQL server and its canonical organization, workspace, legal entity, customers, fiscal periods, accounts and journals. It records service revenue; it does not create stock movements.

Create an active customer through the existing Accounts Receivable customer form, or load an existing customer. Define one to sixteen services with exact quantity text, integer minor-unit prices and integer discount basis points. The explicit policy rounds the discounted unit price HALF_UP to minor units, then rounds quantity × net unit price HALF_UP. The quotation preserves gross price, discount, net price, line totals, currency-policy metadata and digest. Tax is zero in this bounded workflow.

The maker submits the quotation. A different current human approves terms and discounts. Record the customer's accepted order reference inside quotation validity, then the genuine service completion reference and business date. Fulfillment cannot precede the quotation date.

Prepare the invoice using the current open fiscal period, functional-currency journal, Asset receivable account and Income revenue account. The native AR invoice is submitted and the two-line GL draft is captured in the same transaction. An independent reviewer approves the native customer credit exposure and validates the exact GL snapshot. A poster different from the preparer publishes the retained effect. Failed financial participants roll back the enclosing sales command.

Prepare full collection only for the completely unpaid invoice. Capture a receipt number, date, cash journal and Asset cash account. The independent reviewer validates the pending cash/AR effect. The poster then creates the actual native AR receipt and its one full allocation, publishes the reviewed cash/AR GL and advances the sale to Paid within one outer ACID transaction. No Posted receipt or Paid allocation is exposed by preparation or review.

Collection preparation reserves the canonical receipt reference before capturing its financial plan. Native AR uniqueness covers the tenant and workspace, including sibling legal entities. New Sales references contain 1–64 canonical characters: an English letter or digit first, followed by English letters, digits, dot, underscore or hyphen. Python converts letters to uppercase and verifies the resulting reference remains within that limit; SQL independently enforces the same retained ASCII contract. Existing and independent native AR Unicode references retain their established canonical bytes and replay behavior. A previously posted native receipt, another Sales capture, or a hidden sibling-entity owner of the same reference rejects preparation atomically. Choose another reference after the known rejection. Preparing the collection never fabricates a native receipt.

The shared namespace uses the existing forced-RLS `ar_idempotency_keys` table, with explicit `workspace_id`, the closed scope `sales_receipt_name_v1:{workspace_id}` and the canonical receipt reference as its key. The retained JSON contains only schema version, owner kind and opaque owner ID. It contains no amounts, customer details or financial parameters. Ordinary AR replay scopes and envelopes remain unchanged. New native receipt insertion claims that same namespace; direct forged reservations, key updates/deletes/reparenting and native receipt renames/deletes cannot detach a retained owner. Deferred OLD/NEW source checks enforce final coordinated Sales/native closure. Customer, namespace and invoice locks are acquired in the existing native order so capture and posting can retry without a lock inversion.

Migration 0110 backfills valid captured Sales namespaces first, then unowned native receipt namespaces, using persisted receipt bytes and explicit workspace attribution. Reservation creation timestamps come from the actual Sales collection capture command or native receipt, so reconstruction is deterministic and existing key timestamps remain unchanged. It validates all retained owners and fails closed on a conflicting or malformed namespace. A valid Paid Sales receipt retains its Sales owner. The migration uses the already required forced-RLS administrator; runtime functions remain invoker functions with no RLS bypass. An empty Sales downgrade removes only this closed reserved-key scope and its guard functions/triggers, while preserving native receipts and ordinary invoice/receipt replay keys. A populated Sales downgrade continues to refuse removal.

Use the retained command after any lost response or 5xx. Its actor, selected scope, expected version, reason, route and nested financial request remain frozen in browser memory. A modified command identifier, actor or content is rejected. Refresh current state after a known rejection. Current persisted authority is checked before recovery.

Every version retains one immutable command, event and audit reference. PostgreSQL guards verify quotation pricing/digest, selected scope, source ancestry, review and final native AR/GL closure. Direct history mutations and incomplete stage publication fail. A populated downgrade refuses to discard these sources. Before upgrading, preserve a verified backup; rollback a populated deployment through its reviewed restore procedure.

The sales document owns its native invoice and both financial plans. InvoicePrepared requires a Submitted, unallocated AR invoice and an unreviewed financial plan. InvoiceReviewed requires Approved AR and reviewed, unposted revenue. Invoiced and both collection preparation stages require posted revenue and an entirely unallocated Approved invoice. CollectionPrepared requires an unreviewed cash plan; CollectionReviewed requires reviewed, unposted cash. Paid requires both posted financial effects and exactly one full allocation from the owned Posted receipt. Deferred reverse checks on Finance plans, reviews, links and commands and on native AR invoices, receipts and allocations validate the owner at the outer transaction boundary. Generic Finance review/post, native invoice approval and partial/full external allocation cannot independently advance a sales-owned source. Use the Sales commands to coordinate these effects. Unowned native AR and Finance sources retain their existing workflows.

Restricted PostgreSQL roles that mutate native AR or operational Finance need SELECT on `reconforge.sales_revenue_documents` for these invoker checks, even when their sources are unowned. Grant that read dependency explicitly under the existing tenant/workspace/organization/entity RLS. It does not grant Sales table INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE, any Sales application permission, ownership or BYPASSRLS. Audit the role's effective DML privileges separately; keep those false unless the role actually executes the Sales workflow.

Native AR receipt writers also require SELECT and INSERT on `reconforge.ar_idempotency_keys`, their existing replay-key dependency. The namespace bridge adds no UPDATE/DELETE requirement, broader scope grant, table ownership or bypass privilege. Verify legacy operator grants explicitly when upgrading and check that Sales table DML remains false for operators executing only unowned AR workflows.

The reviewed quotation, native invoice and collection receipt preserve the same captured currency, precision, rounding policy, registry version/digest and publication provenance. A later registry or customer policy migration cannot reinterpret quoted minor units: invoice preparation fails atomically before publishing an AR or GL plan. Native PostgreSQL source hooks also reject a linked monetary interpretation that differs from the quotation. A policy change requires a new reviewed quotation.

Stock delivery, cost-of-sales, service credits, refunds, partial collection, taxes and foreign-currency settlement require separate completed workflows. Their absence is shown in the interface and manifest. The module remains experimental pending the integrated native/API/browser/restore gates.
