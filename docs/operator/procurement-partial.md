# Partial stock procurement

Use `/procurement-partial` in Studio with the configured PostgreSQL server. Select an authorized workspace and organization/entity. The reference selectors use existing suppliers, items, stock locations, FIFO policies, open periods, journals and permitted manual posting accounts. They require real configured masters; no demonstration document is generated or required.

Create a single-line purchase order with positive exact quantity and integer minor-unit unit price. Submit it, then have another canonical human identity approve it. The creator and approver identities are retained separately even where a user's ID differs from their username. Each mutation requires current persisted permissions, scoped amount authority and recent step-up.

Prepare a partial receipt with its quantity, actual date and fiscal period. Prepared receipts reserve order capacity, including before their review or posting. Have a second canonical human identity review it, then a third human identity receive it. The poster must differ from both the retained preparer and reviewer. The operation commits the actual inventory receipt, FIFO layer, reviewed inventory/clearing GL effect and native AP goods receipt together. A downstream AP failure rolls back all stock, FIFO, financial and owner acknowledgement writes.

Match a partial supplier invoice only against posted received quantities. The exact unit price comes from the purchase order; partial invoicing can have negative native quantity variance without a price or total variance. Matched invoices reserve that quantity immediately, so concurrent or unapproved invoices cannot consume the same received stock. An independent approver approves each invoice. Prepare its clearing-to-AP accrual, have a second canonical human identity review it, then have a third human identity post it before payment. Preparation, review and posting must be pairwise distinct for each accrual.

The retained native receipt and accrual plans, reviews and posting links supply the displayed preparer, reviewer and poster IDs. Studio disables receipt/accrual publication for their preparer or reviewer, and installment review for its preparer and posting for its preparer or reviewer. Current human authority, exact scope, permissions and recent step-up are verified again by the server. A reviewer publication request returns `409 procurement_partial_duties_conflict` before posting; forcing the native two-person participant and owner writes through a lower-level adapter is rejected by deferred `procurement_partial_owner_phase` closure and rolls back the entire effect. These additional three-person rules apply to this partial procurement owner; the standalone native engines and older full procurement owner retain their existing policies. Historical preparer/reviewer IDs remain immutable and readable after later account deactivation; the executing poster must currently be an authorized human.

For example, an order for ten units at 1,200 minor units can receive four units on October 3, accrue an invoice for three units, then receive six units on October 4 and accrue a second invoice for seven units. The actual posted stock cost becomes 12,000 minor units and the two AP accruals become 3,600 and 8,400. No fractional minor-unit product is silently rounded.

The retained FIFO engine orders movements by business date and its immutable movement number. A receipt dated before an already approved valuation is refused. Multiple receipts on the same business date may also be refused when their generated movement numbers sort before an existing approved movement. Enter the actual receipt date in Studio; the module neither changes a supplied date nor relaxes chronology to force a posting. The initial complete cycle uses consecutive real business dates, and same-day sequencing remains a shared-engine limitation.

Reviewed payment installments use the shared `/financial-installments` contract, an optional settlement extension installed after this module at migration `0115_pg_financial_installments`. Partial receipt, invoice and accrual ownership requires migration `0114_pg_procurement_partial`; Studio/AP partial settlement additionally requires the installed extension and its own acceptance gates. Its persisted plans remain discoverable on the invoice after changing human sessions. Preparation captures an exact amount, invoice version and current residual; review and posting remain separate human operations. Posting atomically creates the AP/cash GL effect and immutable native AP payment link. The invoice remains `Approved` while its residual is positive and becomes `Paid` only when the exact cumulative allocation equals its total. This module reads the resulting allocation history and outstanding balance; the shared installment owner controls payment phases.

The Studio retains the exact prepared request, command ID and expected version after a transport failure, server failure or invalid acknowledgement. Retry that command unchanged. A known conflict requires reloading the current source before preparing a new command. Do not create a replacement key for an operation whose outcome is unknown.

The bounded contract supports one untracked stock line, the supplier/entity functional currency, zero tax, at most 32 receipts and 32 invoices per order. Receipt and invoice numbers use `PPR-<order number>-<sequence>` and `PPI-<order number>-<sequence>`. Orders support at most 40 characters to leave room for the native document suffix. Quantities use the item's supported scale, at most six decimals, and must yield exact minor units when multiplied by the retained unit price.

All four partial procurement tables use forced canonical hierarchy RLS. Native source and participant mutations run invoker-security deferred checks in both OLD and NEW directions. Direct SQL cannot advance approval, review, posting or native source cardinality independently of the retained owner command. Reserved future source numbers must also be absent before atomic capture. Supplier returns, receipt reversal and payment reversal are refused until a separate integrated return/debit contract exists.

Runtime roles need DML only for their assigned engines and the partial owner tables. Native AP/Inventory/Operational Finance invoker guards that reference partial ownership also require finite `SELECT` on `procurement_partial_orders`, `procurement_partial_receipts`, `procurement_partial_invoices` and `procurement_partial_commands`; these dependency reads do not authorize DML on the owner tables and do not bypass RLS. Studio installment discovery reads `financial_installment_plans`, `financial_installment_reviews` and `financial_installment_links`; its invoker-security source verifier additionally reads `financial_installment_commands`. All these reads remain under their forced scope policies. Each role and deployment must explicitly provision these reads; the application does not grant privileges on demand.

The migration is additive. Empty downgrade is supported; a populated downgrade refuses to erase operational or financial evidence. Verify a pre-upgrade PostgreSQL backup before installation and use forward repairs for retained history. Final sprint acceptance must independently prove the full migration head, native source closure, real authenticated API/Studio cycle, recovery and the shared installment contract. Module maturity remains `experimental`; these controls do not establish banking readiness or external certification.
