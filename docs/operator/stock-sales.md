# Governed product sales

This experimental PostgreSQL slice owns one non-lot stock item from a commercial order through reservation, independently reviewed FIFO issue and COGS, native receivable/revenue, and one full cash settlement. It uses existing inventory, valuation, finance posting, receivable and operational finance engines. Runtime acceptance is pending the current integrated gates; the backend checkpoint alone does not establish successful operation.

Select the exact workspace, organization and legal entity. Create an order using its active customer, inventory item, warehouse and nonnegative location. Quantity and price use exact text and minor units. The explicit discount policy rounds the discounted unit price HALF_UP to minor units, then the extended quantity value. Tax is explicitly zero. Order and receipt numbers use at most 64 ASCII letters, digits, hyphen, underscore or dot and are retained uppercase.

The creator submits; a different authenticated human approves the terms. Reserve the full quantity. Prepare an issue against the actual FIFO policy and accounting period. Every selected cost layer retains its native row version, remaining quantity and value. The existing allocator determines exact HALF_EVEN cost. One prepared issue owns an entity/item until it completes or is cancelled; other orders may reserve available stock, but cannot prepare another issue for that item meanwhile. Native outbound commands cannot consume this frozen claim independently.

A different human validates the actual Manual COGS journal. Delivery commits the native stock movement, exact FIFO consumption, approved native valuation and existing GL posting in one outer transaction. The journal debits the policy COGS account and credits the item's inventory account. Posting cannot be published independently of its stock owner. Before delivery, cancellation releases the reservation and voids the independently reviewed unposted journal. A prepared COGS draft must be independently reviewed before cancellation; posted history requires a complete inverse, which is outside this slice.

Prepare the native AR invoice after delivery, review independently, then post revenue through Operational Finance. Capture the cash receipt number before collection review. The existing workspace-wide opaque receipt namespace is extended to StockSales in 0113, preserving generic AR and prior service-sales semantics. Collection atomically publishes the one full native receipt/allocation and the reviewed cash GL posting. No partial allocations, credit notes, returns, FX or tax calculation are claimed.

Every write carries an exact command identifier, source version, reason, actor and scope. Preserve the submitted packet when acknowledgement is lost and retry it unchanged. A changed packet or actor using the same identifier is rejected. SQL checks current source evidence and native phases at outer commit, including native writes made outside the owner API. Scope RLS remains forced and never uses a security-definer bypass.

Install with an administrator through Alembic head. Generic native inventory, finance and receivable roles need their existing engine privileges plus SELECT on stock_sales_orders and stock_sales_issue_claims for the finite reverse owner lookup. The StockSales owner also needs SELECT on its reservations, events and commands, INSERT/UPDATE on orders, reservations and claims, INSERT on immutable events/commands, and existing native write privileges. Native unrelated modules require no StockSales DML. Operators must apply explicit grants appropriate to their deployment; migrations do not broadly grant privileges.

An empty downgrade restores the exact predecessor receipt namespace functions and removes only StockSales hooks/tables. Any retained order or StockSales name claim refuses downgrade. Preserve and verify a native backup rather than deleting financial history. Capacity and FIFO claims are database business objects; do not reset them manually.

Current gate subjects are exact commercial/FIFO domain cases and actual nonowner PostgreSQL stock-to-cash, separation of duties, detached posting rollback and concurrent reservations. API/Studio/restore evidence belongs to the final integrated source, not this checkpoint.
